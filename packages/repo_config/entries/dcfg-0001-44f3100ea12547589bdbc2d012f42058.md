## Decision Configuration — Standard Shape

**Shape:** a `##` heading stating the decision, a **Date:** line, then **Context**, **Options considered**, **Decision**, **Rationale**, and a closing **Reference:** footer.

**Used here for** decisions that follow a failing or disputed result: a design change in response to a failed check, a requirement parameter revised, a test criterion corrected, or a deliberate waiver. A decision that responds to a result must cite that result's real artifact ID in Context and in the Reference footer, and say whether it expects the affected VCP to go stale.

**Not used for** routine status changes. Those are visible in the VCP and its results.

**Reference:** `repo_config` observation configuration (the result shapes decisions point back to).
