### Flags
1. **Declared ranges are not values.** The two properties are draft min/max ranges with no measured or nominal value. A value-versus-limit test needs a value, so these cannot drive a test yet.
2. **`[TBD]` is a reference, not a number.** The envelope is "defined in [TBD]", a pointer to a definition. It is a different kind of parameter from the numeric limits in VCP-0001.
3. **Subject mismatch.** The requirement text says "assembly", but the model links it to the canister. This VCP assumes the canister stands for the assembly. That is an assumption, not a modeled fact.
4. **Neither property relates to REQ-000018.** Thermal mass and heat-loss coefficient do not bear on packaging.
